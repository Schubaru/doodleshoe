About

N00B Labs is my company for building with AI. I make apps, games and tools, and the Claude Code skills I make them with are yours to take apart.

The name is gamer slang. The noob is the one with no gear and no map, who dies first and presses every button anyway. With AI, that's everybody right now, me included. I'd rather admit it and press the buttons.

So far: Wordsword, N00B Portfolios, 368 Dinos and nine skills. Each one started as a design and ended as software that runs.

Mission

Take the mystery out of AI. Have fun building. Try the new thing.

Demystify AI
Most of what gets said about AI is hype or dread. Building one small, real thing teaches more than either. So every project here shows how it was made.
Build for fun
Fun is the brief, not a bonus. That's how a portfolio ended up with a dinosaur game in it.
Try new things
New model, new tool, new idea: I try it on something real before I have an opinion about it. Being a noob is the job.
