About

N00B Labs is an independent, one-person studio where design and engineering are the same job. Everything it makes ships with the reasoning and the tooling behind it.

Skills developed at N00B Labs give conversational AI a specific job to do well: building a design system, testing every edge case, preparing an app for the App Store. The lab uses them to make its own iOS and web apps and browser games.

Mission

Take the mystery out of AI.

Most of what gets said about AI is hype or dread. Building one small, real thing teaches more than either. So every project here shows how it was made.

Have fun building.

Fun is the brief, not a bonus. That's how a portfolio ended up with a dinosaur game in it.

Try the new thing.

New model, new tool, new idea: I try it on something real before I have an opinion about it. Being a noob is the job.
